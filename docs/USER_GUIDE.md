# Power of Yes: How to Use the App

A walkthrough of the volunteer app for each kind of person who uses it. Written for church staff, no technical background needed.

## Start here: try the app in demo mode

The safest way to learn the app is demo mode: it uses made-up people and events, nothing is sent to anyone, and nothing you do touches real church records. Everything you change stays only in your own web browser.

1. Open this link on a computer, tablet or phone: <https://vmottau.github.io/PowerOfYes/?demo>
2. On the sign-in page, under **Demo mode**, click one of the role buttons: **Volunteer**, **Coordinator**, **Church admin** or **Platform owner**. It fills in a sample email for you.
3. Click **Sign in (demo)**. There is no password and no email to check.
4. To try a different role, click the sign-out icon (an arrow next to the email at the bottom of the menu, or at the top right on a phone), then pick another role.
5. A bar across the top reminds you that you're in demo mode. Click **Reset demo** in that bar any time to put all the sample data back the way it started.

The sample church is called **St. Example Parish**, and the sample volunteers have names like "Example: Ann Example" so nobody mistakes them for real people. A second sample church, Grace Community Church, shows how each church gets its own name and colors.

A good first tour: sign in as **Coordinator**, look around, put a volunteer on an event, then sign out and sign in as **Volunteer** to see what that person sees.

## Who does what

Everyone uses the same website, and the menu shows only what your role needs. Each role can do everything the role above it in this table can, plus more.

| Role | Who it's for | What they can do | Menu they see |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Volunteer | Anyone in the parish who says yes | Fill in their volunteer card, see where they're scheduled, say "Can't make it" | My volunteer page |
| Coordinator | Staff or lead volunteers who schedule people | Find volunteers, type in paper cards, run events, take attendance, keep a follow-up list, download spreadsheets and backups | Home, Volunteers, Events & projects, Follow-ups, My card, Manage, Help |
| Church admin | The person in charge of the app for the church | Invite coordinators, change people's roles, set the church's name, colors and logo | Same as coordinator, with more under Manage |
| Platform owner | The person who runs the website | Add a church to the site and name its first admin | All churches, Help |

Signing in never needs a password. You type your email and the app emails you a one-time link (in demo mode you skip the email). Staff have their own volunteer card too, under **My card**.

## Help inside the app

- **Help** in the menu (the **?** icon at the top on a volunteer's phone) has short answers for each role. Staff can also read the volunteers' answers, so they can help someone over the phone.
- The first time someone signs in, a short **welcome box** on their home page shows three things to start with. **Got it** hides it; **Show the welcome box again** on the Help page brings it back.
- Small **?** buttons next to the trickier controls explain them in a sentence. Click one to open it; click anywhere else or press Escape to close it.

## For volunteers

Volunteers have one page, **My volunteer page**, with their card at the top and their schedule below it. In demo mode, sign in as **Volunteer** to see it.

### Signing up for the first time

1. Open the sign-up link your church shared (in the bulletin, an email or on the website). It looks like `https://vmottau.github.io/PowerOfYes/#/c/st-marys` and shows your church's name and colors.
2. Type your email and click **Email me a sign-in link**. Open the email on the same device and click the link. It works once and expires in an hour.
3. Fill in your volunteer card: name, phone, email, and a check by every ministry and event you'd like to help with. These are the same boxes as the paper card.
4. Click **Save my card**. A coordinator will reach out when something fits.

If you filled out a paper card before, sign in with the same email that's on it. The app connects you to your existing card automatically.

If you sign in without using the church's link, the app asks you to **Open your church's link**. People can only join a church through its link or an invite.

### Changing your card

Click **Edit my card**, change anything, and click **Save my card**.

### Seeing where you're scheduled

Under **Where you're scheduled** you'll see each upcoming event with its date, time, place and any notes from the coordinator. Past events are listed under **Past** with "You served" or another status, and the page thanks you for how many times you've served.

### If you can't make it

1. Next to the event, click **Can't make it**.
2. Optionally add a note, like "Out of town that weekend."
3. Click **Let them know**. Your spot opens up and the coordinators see it right away on their home page.

Changed your mind? Click **I can make it after all** on the same event to get back on the schedule.

## For coordinators

Coordinators land on **Home** after signing in. In demo mode, sign in as **Coordinator** to follow along.

### Home: what needs attention today

Four boxes across the top show how many volunteers you have, how many are new in the last 30 days, how many follow-ups are due, and how many events are coming up. Click any box to jump there. Below them:

- **Can't make it** (in red, only when it applies): volunteers who backed out of an upcoming event, with their note. Click one to open the event and find a replacement.
- **Still need volunteers**: upcoming events with fewer people than they need, with a bar showing, for example, "3 of 6".
- **Follow-ups due**: your to-do calls and emails. Tick the box to mark one done.
- **New volunteers**: people who joined recently, marked "Online" or "Paper card".

### Volunteers: finding the right people

This is the searchable version of the card box.

1. Type in **Search name, phone or email**, or click area buttons like "Pierogies" to see everyone who checked that box. Click more than one area to see people who checked any of them.
2. Narrow it with the contact buttons: **To contact** (nobody has reached out yet), **Contacted**, or **Has login** (signed up online).
3. Each person shows their phone and email (with copy buttons), interests, how many times they've served or missed, and staff notes.
4. **Copy emails** copies every shown address so you can paste them into a group email. **Download spreadsheet** saves the shown list for Excel or Google Sheets. **Print list** prints it.
5. Click **Clear filters** to see everyone again.

### Typing in a paper card

Click **+ Add a paper card**, fill in what's on the card, and click **Save**. You can also add **Staff notes** (only coordinators see them) and tick **We've reached out to this person** once someone has called them. To fix a card later, click **Edit** on it. Volunteers who sign up online appear here on their own.

### Scheduling people for an event

There are two ways to put people on an event.

**From the Volunteers page:** tick the box beside each person (or **Select all shown**), choose the event from the bar that appears at the bottom, and click **Assign**. Choose **+ Create a new event…** in that list to make the event and assign everyone in one step.

**From the event itself:**

1. Open **Events & projects** and click **+ New event or project**. Give it a name, date, time, place, the **Area of interest** it belongs to, how many **Volunteers needed**, and **Notes for volunteers** (what to bring, who's in charge). Volunteers see those notes. Click **Save**.
2. On the event page, under **Add volunteers**, the list starts with people who checked that area, marked "Interested". Untick **Only people who checked …** to see everyone.
3. Tick people and click **Add to roster**. They now see the event on their own page.

### After the event: who came

Open the event (past events are listed under **Past · mark attendance**). For each person on the roster, click **Attended** or **Missed**, or click **Mark all attended** and fix the few who didn't come. This builds each volunteer's "served" count.

On any event you can also **Print sign-in sheet**, **Download roster**, **Copy roster emails**, **Edit details**, or remove someone with the **x**. If someone pressed Can't make it, they show in red with **Put back on roster** in case plans change again.

### Follow-ups: your to-do list

A follow-up is a note like "Call Ann about the pierogi batch, due Friday". Add one with **Follow up** on any volunteer, or **+ New follow-up** on the Follow-ups page. Pick the volunteer, write what needs to happen, choose a due date and **Who will do it**. Overdue ones show first. Tick **Only mine** to see just yours, and tick a follow-up to mark it done.

### Manage: the lists behind the app

- **Ministries & areas**: the checkboxes on the volunteer card, in two groups, Parish ministries and Events & activities. Add one, edit its name or short note, move it up or down, or **Hide** it. Hiding stops offering it on new cards but keeps it on people who already checked it. **Delete** removes it everywhere.
- **People & roles**: who has joined the church and their role, plus the church's **Volunteer sign-up link**. Click **Copy** to share it in the bulletin, by email or as a QR code. Only a church admin can change roles.
- **Backup**: **Download backup (.zip)** saves every volunteer, event, roster and follow-up as spreadsheets. Do this about once a month and keep it with parish records, somewhere only staff can open, because it holds phone numbers and emails.

## For church admins

A church admin can do everything a coordinator can, plus decide who else runs the app and how it looks. In demo mode, sign in as **Church admin**.

### Inviting coordinators

1. Open **Manage → People & roles**.
2. Under **Invite someone**, type their email, choose **Coordinator** or **Church admin**, and click **Send invite**.
3. Their role starts the first time they sign in with that exact email. Until then they're listed as "Waiting to sign in", and you can **Cancel** the invite.

In the list of everyone who has joined, change a person's role with the dropdown, or click the **x** to remove them from the church. You can't remove yourself.

### Sharing the sign-up link

Under **Volunteer sign-up link**, click **Copy** and share it in the bulletin, in an email, on the church website or as a QR code. Anyone who signs up through it joins your church, and no one else's.

### Making it look like your church

Open **Manage → Appearance** to set the church name, the app title, the welcome line on the sign-in page, a main and an accent color (or pick a ready-made palette like Navy or Forest), and a logo (a square image works best). Changes preview right away. Click **Save appearance** to apply them for everyone, or **Undo changes** to go back.

## For the platform owner

Most church staff will never see this role. The platform owner lands on **All churches**, which lists every church on the site. To add one, fill in the church's name, a short **Link name** like `st-marys`, and **Their admin's email**, then click **Add church**. That person becomes the church's admin the first time they sign in. **Copy link** copies a church's sign-up link, **Open** goes into a church, and **Pause** hides a church from sign-up without deleting anything.

## Common questions

**Did I break anything in demo mode?** No. Demo mode only changes sample data in your own browser. Click **Reset demo** to start over.

**A volunteer says they never got the sign-in email.** Ask them to check their spam or junk folder, make sure they typed their email correctly, and try again after a minute. The link expires after an hour and works only once.

**Someone filled out a paper card and then signed up online. Will they be listed twice?** Not if they used the same email address as on the paper card. The app joins the two automatically.

**Why don't I see the Manage or Volunteers pages?** Your account is set as a volunteer. Ask the church admin to change your role under Manage → People & roles.

**Can a volunteer see other volunteers' phone numbers or staff notes?** No. Volunteers see only their own card and schedule.

**Someone signed in and was asked to "Open your church's link".** They signed in without using the church's sign-up link. Send them the link from Manage → People & roles.

**Does the app cost anything?** No. The website and database run on free plans.

**Who do I ask for help?** Volunteers and staff should ask their coordinator first. If the coordinator can't solve it, they contact the person who runs the site.
